WEBSITE · CLOUDLAB STUDIO

Danny4686.com Is Live

A new home for CloudLab Studio, Rocket Tycoon, browser games, and the memories still ahead.

A place for everything I build

Danny4686.com started as a clean personal homepage, but it has grown into the home of CloudLab Studio. It now brings my projects, community, profiles, and creative work together in one place.

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages and connected to my custom domain. That means it stays online without my computer running and updates automatically whenever new files are published to the repository.

Built to keep growing

The main page introduces who I am and what I build. The new Browser Games area gives small games their own home, beginning with Snake. The Studio Journal creates a timeline where updates and memories can be saved by date.

Journal posts can use normal images, animated GIFs, and muted looping videos. Videos automatically play when they are visible on screen and pause after the reader scrolls past them.

Pieces of the studio

What comes next

This is only the first journal entry. New posts can document game updates, website changes, trips, artwork, milestones, or anything else worth remembering. Each post gets its own title, description, thumbnail, date, tags, and permanent page.
